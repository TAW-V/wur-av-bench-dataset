Camera intrinsics of the viewpoint data

Camera:      Intel RealSense L515, colour camera
Image size:  960 x 540 pixels (width x height), the same for all 25 plants

fx = 685.5028
fy = 685.6410
cx = 485.3596
cy = 270.7331

Pinhole model without distortion terms. A point (x, y, z) in the camera optical frame
(x right, y down, z forward, in metres) projects to the pixel

    u = fx * x / z + cx    (column)
    v = fy * y / z + cy    (row)

The values were estimated from the organized point clouds, which are aligned to the colour
images. They project each point onto its own pixel with a median error of about 0.25 pixel.
