Layout of the viewpoint data

Every viewpoint of a plant has three files with the same time stamp (nanoseconds):

    <stamp>_color.jpeg   colour image, 960 x 540 pixels
    <stamp>_b.pcd        organized point cloud, aligned to the colour image
    <stamp>_pose.txt     camera pose


Point cloud (<stamp>_b.pcd)

- Binary PCD (version 0.7) with the fields x y z rgb: x, y and z as float32 in metres, rgb as a
  packed uint32 colour (0x00RRGGBB).
- Organized, with one point per image pixel (518400 points). The header says WIDTH 540 and
  HEIGHT 960, but these are swapped: the points are stored row by row as 540 rows of 960 points,
  in the same order as the image pixels.
- Coordinates are in the camera optical frame: x right, y down, z forward along the viewing
  direction.
- Pixels without depth are NaN.
- The VIEWPOINT field of the header holds the same camera pose as <stamp>_pose.txt, in the
  order tx ty tz qw qx qy qz.


Camera pose (<stamp>_pose.txt)

- A printed ROS TransformStamped with frame_id "world" and child_frame_id
  "camera_color_optical_frame".
- The translation (x, y, z, in metres) and the rotation quaternion (x, y, z, w) give the pose of
  the camera in the world frame: a point p in the camera optical frame lies at R p + t in the
  world frame, with R the rotation matrix of the quaternion and t the translation.
- The world frame is the frame of the ground truth in gt/ and of detection_filter_params.json.

See camera_intrinsics.txt for the projection between points and pixels.
